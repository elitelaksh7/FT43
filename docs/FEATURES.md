## Enhanced Features

- **AI Categorization Engine**: Automatically assigns categories to transactions and continuously learns from user corrections to deliver increasingly personalized and accurate results over time.

- **OCR Bill Scanner**: Instantly extracts line-items, totals, taxes, and merchant details from receipts and bills, providing an immediate, detailed analysis that flows straight into your expense tracking.

- **Personal Price Tracker**: Tracks prices for items you frequently buy, highlights trends, and alerts you when prices drop or exceed your usual spending range.

- **Real-time Analytics (WebSockets)**: Live updates for dashboards, budgets, and alerts delivered over WebSockets, so charts, balances, and insights reflect changes the moment they happen.

- **Intelligent Nudges**: Context-aware recommendations that help you stick to budgets, avoid unnecessary fees, and optimize recurring expenses.

- **Gamification**: Streaks, achievements, and milestones that reward consistent tracking and smart financial habits to boost long-term engagement.
